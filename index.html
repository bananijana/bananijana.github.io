About

I am a geology graduate with an M.Sc. in Applied Geology from Presidency University, Kolkata. I started with field mapping in West Bengal and Jharkhand, and grew into mineral exploration, remote sensing and geospatial modelling.

Since August 2026 I have been a Junior Geologist at Shanvi Resources in Bhubaneswar, working on exploration, borehole correlation, cross-sections and resource estimation.

My interests centre on mineral exploration and resource estimation, supported by geological interpretation, remote sensing and GIS, AutoCAD, Python-based analysis, geological modelling, and clear technical reporting.

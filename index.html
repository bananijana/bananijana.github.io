01

Mapping the subsurface

I'm a Jr. Geologist at Shanvi Resources in Bhubaneswar, working in mineral exploration and mineral resource estimation. I use AutoCAD, ArcGIS, QGIS and Global Mapper for borehole correlation, geological modelling and technical reporting.

Before this, I completed an M.Sc. in Applied Geology at Presidency University, with a dissertation on river-aquifer interaction in the Hooghly–Rupnarayan Interfluve of the Lower Gangetic Plain. My field training includes uranium exploration with the Atomic Minerals Directorate in the Singhbhum Shear Zone, and field mapping in Purulia, Galudih, Kharia and Maithon.

I also write Python for geoscience, from drillhole and ore-body modelling to ERT inversion and machine-learning classification, and I have presented research at national and international conferences.
